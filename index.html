About

Research grounded in the concrete transformations of work.

My background combines management research, history and professional experience in facility management. I currently teach and conduct research in a business school.

This dual perspective informs an approach attentive to spatiality, work practices and the human dynamics of organizational change. My work takes the form of academic articles, books, case studies and contributions aimed at broader audiences.

Research

Three lenses on contemporary organizations.

Research that connects space, behaviour and history to shed light on managerial decisions.

01

Workplaces

Flex office, coworking, remote work and office design: how does spatiality shape interactions, engagement and employee experience?

02

Organizational transformation

I examine the effects of new management practices, resistance to change and the ways organizations adapt and evolve.

03

History & management

Drawing on the humanities and organizational history offers new ways to understand contemporary management challenges.
